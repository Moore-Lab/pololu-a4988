Lens Z wiring

Two A4988 carriers drive Z1 and Z2, both pushing one lens along one axis, with a MIN and a MAX limit switch on each motor. Check the build against the figures, then work through the checklist at the bottom before motor power goes on.

⚠

Never plug or unplug a motor while its driver has motor power. It destroys A4988s. Set the current limit with the motors disconnected (checklist, stage C).

The whole rig

Two identical channels. The two channels share ENABLE, the 5 V logic supply, the motor supply and one ground. Everything else is per channel.

One channel, every wire

Channel Z1. Z2 is identical except STEP comes from D5 and DIR from D4. ENABLE (D6), 5 V and ground are the same wires, shared.

On the carrier itself

The same connections in the real pin order of your board, the Pololu green A4988 carrier, seen from the component side with the VREF pot at the bottom.

Each limit switch

Four of these: D7 Z1 MIN, D8 Z1 MAX, D9 Z2 MIN, D10 Z2 MAX. Use the NC terminal, so a cut wire reads as a tripped switch.
